Nxt Phase AI04
How it works

One owned data layer. One bouncer. Verified blueprints.

Permissions sit above a deliberately dumb database. The blueprint is versioned data the agent changes safely, never freeform code.

Nxt Phase AI05
The insight

The schema is trivial. The state machine is the moat.

Anyone can copy a table of fields. What Salesforce and SAP actually sell, hidden under the UI, is the operational logic: which states are legal, who may move between them, what must always hold. Temple makes that explicit, and verifies it.

What you don't sell

A deals table. CRUD. Claude writes it in an afternoon. No defensibility, no value.

What you do sell

A verified blueprint: states, transitions, invariants, policy, and a multi-level verifier that proves they hold against messy real data.
