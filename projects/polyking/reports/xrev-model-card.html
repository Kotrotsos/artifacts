Intended use & scope

Intended: research signal for Polymarket "Bitcoin Up or Down" 5-minute markets, and for studying short-horizon crypto microstructure. Decisions are selective: it abstains far more often than it bets.

Out of scope: longer horizons (the daily model is a separate system), other assets, and any use as financial advice. It is not a high-accuracy oracle; the edge is small by design.

Not financial advice. Paper trading only. Realized profit depends on the price you can actually fill at, which is the one thing the backtest cannot prove.

Limitations & honest caveats

The edge is thin. About 1.6 points over a coin toss. A 5-minute move is mostly random; this finds a faint, real tilt, not a strong predictor.

Fills are unproven. Backtest assumes entry near 0.50. If the market already prices the same momentum, the edge shrinks. The live panel measures this for real.

One idea failed. Round-number magnetism contributed almost nothing. The lift came from mean reversion plus micro-range position and order-flow persistence.

Decay risk. As these markets mature and more participants arrive, a small microstructure edge can be arbitraged away. Needs ongoing monitoring.

2 How it works

Anatomy of one bet

You commit at the open of the window using only what is known then. The window resolves 5 minutes later on the sign of the move. The model predicts that sign.

The pipeline

The five signal families

Mean reversion drives edge

How far price has stretched above or below its 1-hour and 3-hour averages. Stretched up tends to pull back.

Micro-range position novel, worked

Where price sits inside the recent high-to-low band. At the top it leans down, at the bottom it leans up.

Order-flow persistence novel, worked

Whether aggressive buyers or sellers dominated recently (taker imbalance). Has short memory that carries forward.

Overreaction + volatility

The last move z-scored by recent volatility. Big bars snap back. Volatility regime gates how strong the effect is.

Round-number magnetism flopped

Distance to nearest 500 and 1000 dollar levels. The idea I was most excited about, and it contributed almost nothing.

Time of day

Cyclical encoding of the time of day and day of week, so the model can switch behavior by session.

The bet gate, interactive

A 51% predictor that bets every window loses to the spread. XREV only bets when expected value at the real ask price is positive. Drag the sliders.

Expected value per 1 dollar share = P(win) minus the price you pay. The gate requires EV above +0.01 to bet, leaving room for being wrong about the edge.

3 Performance

Accuracy by month (whole year)

Bounces around 51.5% with normal noise. The last point (June) is a 1.5-day sliver and is statistical noise, not decay.

Accuracy by volatility regime

The edge is broad, not a one-regime fluke. Positive in calm, low, elevated and high volatility.

Does the edge survive real prices? Break-even analysis

Return per bet as the market gets smarter (k = fraction of the signal the market already prices). Where each line crosses zero is the break-even. At a 2-cent spread the market must already know 58% of the signal to erase the edge.

0c spread (be 0.87) 1c spread (be 0.73) 2c spread (be 0.58) 3c spread (be 0.43)
Bottom line. XREV is a small, real, leak-free directional edge (about 51.6% over a full year, z = 4.9), broad across regimes and stable over time, made tradeable by a strict expected-value gate. The open question is not the prediction, it is the fill: whether live Polymarket prices stay loose enough to capture it. That is what the live dashboard panel now measures.
