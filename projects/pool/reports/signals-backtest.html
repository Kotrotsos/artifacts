Backtesting the signal layer

A four-agent team stress-tested the v4 news-signal layer. The honest answer: it is a harmless editorial overlay, not a validated accuracy gain.

The team

AnalystDesigned 4 valid tests given that 2026 news can't be reconstructed historically.
ResearcherGraded each signal category against real tournament history.
DeveloperBuilt scripts/backtest_v4.py, ran all tests on held-out matches.
ReviewerAdversarially audited the code and caught a test-set leak.

What can even be tested

The specific 2026 signals (Iran's visa crisis, Ghana's coaching change) cannot be turned into structured scores for matches played in 2014, so we cannot directly measure "did these signals improve held-out accuracy." Instead the team tested what is testable: the mechanism (does a nudge of this size help or hurt), reconstructable proxies for the signal categories, and tournament robustness. Metric: Ranked Probability Score (RPS, lower better) on 3,431 real matches from 2023–2026 the model never trained on. Baseline RPS 0.1660.

Does history support these signals?

Signal categoryHistorical evidenceNote
Key injuriesSTRONGQuantified (Neymar 2014 dropped Brazil's title odds ~9pp). The one channel worth real weight.
Squad turmoil / revoltsWEAK-MODERATEFrance 2010, NED 2012 are real but tiny-n and reverse-causal (losing breeds turmoil stories).
Late manager changeWEAKUsually a symptom, not a cause; direction ambiguous.
Momentum / warm-up formNONE-WEAKArgentina 2022 lost its opener after a 36-game unbeaten run, then won it. Already in the rating.
Off-field / geopolitical crisisNONESign isn't even consistent. Pure post-hoc narrative.

The four tests (honest results)

TestResultVerdict
Placebo — shuffle the real magnitudes onto random teams (300×) Mean ΔRPS +0.00005; 43% of random shuffles "improve". The real assignment scores −0.00029 (z≈−1.3, p≈0.09) — not significant. NOISE magnitude is harmless
Magnitude sweep — does any scale of a momentum nudge help? On an honest VAL→TEST split, ≈−0.0001 at the production scale (within noise) and harmful (+0.0029) by scale 0.12. Random nudges always hurt. FLAT only tiny scale is safe
Proxy flags — reconstructable momentum/coaching/absence flags in a GBM ΔRPS +0.00001 — within noise. NULL adds nothing (like form/H2H)
Tournament robustness — perturb 2018/2022 at signal magnitude France 2018 stays #6 (~5.1%), Argentina 2022 stays #2 (~23%). ROBUST won't blow up the forecast
Peer review caught a false positive. The developer's first pass reported a clear win — a momentum nudge cutting RPS by 0.0026. The reviewer found it was an artifact: the proxy was built and its scale chosen on the test set, with look-ahead aggregation. Re-run honestly, the gain disappears and at large scale flips to harm. This is exactly why the team has a skeptic — and why no number here is sold without an out-of-sample check.

Verdict

No test cleared statistical significance. The signal layer's magnitude is harmless at the small production scale, the reconstructable proxies add nothing beyond the validated rating, and the forecast is robust to it. That makes v4 defensible as a clearly-labelled qualitative overlay — a transparent way to encode current-conditions news — but not as an accuracy improvement backed by evidence.

Recommendation to the project:
  • Treat the backtested v3 (RPS 0.166) as the engine of record; present v4 as an editorial prior, clearly labelled, not "more accurate".
  • Shrink the cap well below ±0.15 — anything ≥0.05 degrades accuracy on honest data.
  • Drop the momentum and crisis contributions (no evidence); concentrate weight on injuries, the only channel with quantified backing.
