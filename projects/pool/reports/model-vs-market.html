Model vs Market

Our forecast against Polymarket's $1.25B market on the 2026 World Cup winner — and what the gap taught us.

Calibration vs Polymarket

Polymarket aggregates capital across thousands of traders into per-team championship probabilities. We treat it as a market-implied benchmark (after de-vigging the 3.7% overround). Comparing across the top 16 by joint visibility:

+0.57v3 correlation (Pearson r)
3.44ppv3 mean abs error
+0.67v5 correlation (Pearson r)
2.93ppv5 mean abs error
v5 is better calibrated to market than v3 (r +0.57→+0.67, MAE 3.44→2.93pp). The improvement comes from two added layers — see "What changed" below.

What changed in v5

  • Confederation calibration — empirically derived from 2,057 cross-confederation matches since 2010 (data/confederation_calibration.json). The bias is small (±0.02 log-goal for most confederations; OFC is the outlier at −0.064 on n=116). Notable finding: CONMEBOL is NOT over-rated by Elo in cross-confederation play — it actually slightly over-performs. So the model's Argentina/Colombia lean is not a closed-pool artifact; it's structural information the market prices in (squad/star quality) that pure-results models miss.
  • Squad-depth prior — z-scored top-11 market value mapped to a capped ±0.08 log-goal nudge (data/squad_depth.json). Rewards the deep European talent pools the market visibly prices. Biggest positive shifts: Spain, England, France, Brazil, Germany, Portugal. Biggest negatives: Iran, Iraq, Jordan, Qatar.
  • Both layers are bounded and transparent; the confederation piece is historically derived, the squad-depth piece is a current-only prior (same status as v2's value/injury layer).

Where the model still disagrees with the market

v5 closes some of the gap but not all. The persistent disagreements:

  • Argentina — we still have them as title favourite; market has them sixth. Possible market reasons we don't price: post-2022 hangover, Messi at 38, narrative discount on the reigning champion.
  • France — market still ~10pp above us. Even after squad-depth, our results-only ratings don't fully capture France's elite player-level quality.
  • Colombia — we still have them ~7pp above market. Strong CONMEBOL form is real (the empirical confederation check confirms it); the market may be under-pricing it.

The honest reading: the market has information about player quality, narrative, and recent club football that pure international-results models can't see. v5 is calibrated; "right" is unknowable until June.
