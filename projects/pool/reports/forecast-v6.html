Methodology

v6 stacks six layers, each tuned and then locked before forecasting.

  1. Team Elo from 49,000 international matches. Standard Elo update with margin scaling and recency decay, applied to every full international since the dataset start. Produces the base rating each team enters the tournament with.
  2. Per-player goal-Elo from 47,000 goalscorer records. Every recorded international goal updates a per-player offensive rating relative to the opposition defence. Defenders, midfielders, and goalkeepers receive their own derived ratings from positional context and minutes.
  3. Best-XI weighted squad strength. For each nation we assemble the best available XI given current call-ups and form, then compute weighted averages over GK, DEF, MID, ATT plus top-4 player ratings to produce a squad strength score against a 1750 baseline.
  4. Confederation calibration. A log-goal shift per confederation, fitted on out-of-confederation matches, corrects for systematic over-rating or under-rating. UEFA and CONMEBOL get small positive shifts, CONCACAF, AFC, OFC small negative.
  5. Host advantage. A pure Elo bonus of +80 applied to each of the three hosts, Canada, Mexico, and the United States, decaying through knockout rounds.
  6. Locked simulation params. Goal-base lambda 1.35, superiority divisor 210.0, minimum lambda 0.18, draw boost 0.05, squad weight 0.8. Selected by lowest sum of champion ranks across the last three editions.
