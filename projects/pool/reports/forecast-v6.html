Bug findings

    Refinement journey

    Getting from raw Elo to v6.2 took 36+ hyperparameter combinations across three sweep families (12 candidates in sweep 2, 10 rounds in sweep 3, 12 candidates in the v6.2 sweep), plus four explicit refinement rounds after the leading candidate was selected. The full record is committed in data/v6/. Each refinement either added a signal, changed a weight, or both.

    Adding per-player goal Elo, online attack/defense, WC tournament bias, recent-champion credit, strong-opponent form, and a defending-champion penalty progressively improved the backtest. The aggressive lock (candidate t03) hit rank-1 in 1 of 4 backtests (2022 Argentina at 56.9%) and rank-2 on both 2010 Spain and 2014 Germany. France 2018 remained at rank 3 because France's pre-tournament Elo of 2027 sat over 100 points below Brazil, Germany, and Spain, and the model would not push France to rank-1 without overfitting on a single-WC feature.

    The original goal asked for a perfect rank-1 sweep on the last 3 World Cups. We did not reach that. The closest honest result, given strict no-peeking and no per-tournament hand-tuning, is the locked state shown above. The production-calibrated mode trades historical fit for live calibration; the backtest-fit mode reverses the trade.

    Methodology, nine signals, final weights

    Team Elo
    World Football Elo from 49,257 historical matches
    base
    Per-player goal Elo
    Opponent-adjusted scoring rating from 47,602 goalscorer records, 14,842 scorers
    data/v6/player_ratings.json
    Online attack/defense
    Bivariate-Poisson team rating walked through history
    w_attack=0.1, w_defense=0.1
    WC tournament bias
    Per-team residual vs Elo in past 8 WC matches
    w_wc_bias=5.0, neg_only=True
    Recent champion credit
    Finalist/SF credit at Euro, Copa, AFCON, AC, Gold Cup, WC last 4 years
    w_recent_champ=2.5, scale=70.0
    Strong-opponent form
    PPG vs Elo&ge;1900 opponents in last 24m (friendlies excluded)
    w_strong_opp_form=3.0
    Defending-champion penalty
    Flat Elo subtraction; reflects post-1998 curse (3 of 7 group-stage exits)
    penalty=300.0 Elo
    Best-XI squad strength
    Best XI weighted average from 1,227 active player ratings
    w_squad=0
    Confederation calibration
    Empirical log-goal shift from 2,057 cross-confederation matches since 2010
    scale=80.0
    Host advantage
    Applied to USA, Canada, Mexico in 2026 forecast
    0.0 Elo (aggressive); 70 Elo (calibrated)
    Weights shown are the aggressive backtest-fit lock. The calibrated forecast uses mild near-default weights (w_wc_bias=0, w_recent_champ=0, w_scorer=0, w_strong_opp_form=0, w_attack/def=0.3, defending_champion_penalty=0, w_squad=0.3) so the live 2026 distribution stays market-aligned.
