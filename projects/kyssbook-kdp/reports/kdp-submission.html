02The one decision: ink & paper

The interior is designed in color (green section bands, blue links, syntax-highlighted code). At 8 × 10 with 613 pages this matters, because KDP's color tiers cap page count and color print costs are steep. Pick a lane before you start the paperback.

Recommended Black & white interior, white paper. Upload the same color PDF; KDP prints it in grayscale (code stays readable, shades stand in for syntax color). No page-count limit issue (max 828 pp). Lowest cost, sellable at ~$34.99. The cover spine is already built for this.
Premium colour Full color print. The only color tier that allows 613 pages (Standard Color caps at 600 at this trim). Per-copy print cost runs into the tens of dollars, forcing a high minimum list price. Choose only if color print is essential, and confirm the price on KDP's calculator first.
Standard colour Not available. 8 × 10 Standard Color is capped at 600 pages; this book is 613. To use it you would shrink the concept banners to drop under 600 (ask and I will do it).

Cover spine check

The wrap is built at 1.380" spine (white paper, 0.002252 in/page × 613). That is correct for Black & White and for Premium Color on white paper. 03Flow A — Kindle eBook

KDP Bookshelf → Create → Kindle eBook. Three tabs: Details, Content, Pricing.

  1. Language English. Book Title and Subtitle from the metadata below. Paste exactly; the subtitle is a separate field.
  2. Author Marco Kotrotsos. Leave contributors empty.
  3. Description paste the listing copy below. KDP accepts light HTML, but plain paragraphs are fine.
  4. Publishing rights "I own the copyright and hold the rights."
  5. Keywords paste the seven below, one per box.
  6. Categories pick the three listed below (Computers / AI, Programming, Software Dev Tools).
  7. Age range / pre-order leave blank · No pre-order.
  8. Content → Manuscript upload claude-code-unstoppable.epub. No need to enable DRM; your call.
  9. Content → Cover upload cover-ebook.jpg. The EPUB already embeds it; uploading the JPG sets the catalog thumbnail.
  10. Preview open the online previewer, spot-check a code page and a chapter opener, then Save.
  11. ISBN none needed for Kindle (Amazon assigns an ASIN).
  12. Pricing $9.99 at the 70% royalty plan, all territories. KDP Select enrollment is optional.
  13. Publish review and submit. Live in up to 72 hours.

04Flow B — Paperback

From the ebook's "+" menu choose Create Paperback so the two link on one product page. Same three tabs.

Read this first: the 6 × 9 trap

KDP defaults the trim size to 6 × 9. This book is 8 × 10. If you leave the default, the previewer throws a wall of errors (interior size mismatch, "image outside margins", "insufficient gutter", wrong cover size) because KDP is squeezing 8×10 pages into a 6×9 frame. None of those are real file problems.

Set trim8 × 10 in in Print Options before anything else.
Cover fileUpload paperback-cover.pdf (17.64 × 10.25, back + spine + front). Not the interior, not a single 8×10 page. At 8×10 trim KDP expects 17.63 × 10.25 and this file matches.
  1. Reuse the metadata title, subtitle, author, description, keywords, categories all match the ebook.
  2. ISBN "Get a free KDP ISBN." The wrap already reserves the back-cover barcode area; KDP overlays the barcode.
  3. Print options → Ink and paper select Black & white interior with white paper (see decision above). Switch to Premium Color only if you accept the higher price.
  4. Trim size → 8 × 10 in (change it from the 6 × 9 default). Bleed "No bleed." Cover finish matte (suits the flat design) or glossy. This is the step everyone misses; set it before uploading anything.
  5. Manuscript upload claude-code-unstoppable-interior.pdf (8 × 10, 613 pp).
  6. Cover choose "Upload a cover you already have (PDF)" and upload the full wrap paperback-cover.pdf (17.64 × 10.25). Not the interior and not a single page; it must be the back+spine+front wrap.
  7. Launch Previewer let KDP run its checks. Confirm no margin or spine warnings, then approve.
  8. Pricing $34.99 (US). KDP shows the printing cost and royalty live; adjust other marketplaces as you like.
  9. Proof first order a printed proof before going live (see final checklist).
  10. Publish.

07Before you hit publish

01Order a printed proof. Check the grayscale code screenshots read well and the spine text sits centered. This is the single most useful step.
02Confirm the spine in the KDP Cover Calculator for your chosen paper. If it differs from 1.380", ping me to regenerate paperback-cover.pdf.
03Run Kindle Previewer on the EPUB once more after upload; flip to a code page and a chapter opener.
04Link the two editions so the paperback and Kindle share one product page (create the paperback from the ebook's menu).
05Hold the price line. If B&W, $34.99 clears a healthy royalty. If you switch to color, recheck the minimum allowed list price before publishing.
