KDP Submission Playbook

Kill Your SaaS Stack — everything you need to publish the eBook, paperback, and hardcover yourself, in order.

You do the clicking; this page has every value to paste and every file to upload. Publish the eBook first, then add the paperback and hardcover as linked formats. Budget ~45 minutes.

0. Before you start one-time

  1. Sign in at kdp.amazon.com.
  2. If this is your first title, finish account setup: the tax interview and bank details. Nothing can be created until that is done.

Tab 2 — Content

  1. Manuscript: upload ebook.epub (validated, epubcheck clean, images embedded). Fall back to ebook.docx only if KDP rejects the EPUB.
  2. Cover: upload cover-ebook.jpg (1600×2560, titled).
  3. ISBN: not needed for eBook (Amazon assigns an ASIN).
  4. Open the Kindle Previewer and check the TOC links, a screenshot, and a diagram.

2. Add the Paperback

From the same title, choose Create Paperback (links it to the eBook). Details copy over; verify them.

Content

Open the Print Previewer and check title/spine alignment, that the barcode placeholder sits bottom-right, and that the screenshots and diagrams read clearly in grayscale. The cover spine is built for exactly 381 pages; if the interior page count ever changes, rebuild the cover with generate_covers.py.

Pricing

Printing cost (US, 6×9 B&W white, 381 pp): $1.00 + 381 × $0.012 = $5.57. Minimum list for 60% royalty is about $9.29.

4. After publishing

  • Claim your author page at authorcentral.amazon.com; upload your photo and the bio below.
  • Verify each live listing: “Look Inside,” categories, and the Buy button.
  • Confirm the three formats are linked on one product page.
