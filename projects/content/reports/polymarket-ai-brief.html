The June market jumped 6.25 points in 24 hours, the May market 2 points. OpenAI is at 3.45% for May, 6.5% for June. xAI is 0.65% and 1.3%.

What this collectively suggests: the market does not see this as a horse race anymore. Traders are pricing in Anthropic owning the leaderboard for two consecutive months and the valuation crossover within the year. Money is moving toward Anthropic, not just commentary.

Why it matters this week: If Anthropic ships anything that confirms the lead, expect a fast repricing higher and a wave of "OpenAI lost the throne" coverage. If OpenAI counters with a model announcement, the May market is the place to watch for the reaction speed.

Theme 2Compute scarcity is the unspoken story

NVIDIA's largest-company odds rose 3.25 points to 88.8% in 24 hours while every other Magnificent-7 candidate sits at single digits or below. At the same time, GPU rental price markets show H200 and B200 indices breaking through previously skeptical price ceilings, with multiple thresholds jumping 20 to 37 points in a single day toward Yes resolution at higher price brackets.

What this collectively suggests: the supply side is tightening, not loosening. The cohort that was supposed to relieve compute pressure (Trainium, TPU v6, MI300X, Chinese alternatives) has not budged the price.

Why it matters this week: Any earnings, capex commentary, or hyperscaler buildout news will be read against this signal. If NVIDIA prints a guide, the markets will move first and the narrative will follow.
