Where money goes

Per-response unit cost

A "response" is one stakeholder completing one interview. Average 10 conversation turns, system prompt cached after turn 1, plus the end-of-response extraction pass that lands the structured fields.

Step Model Input tokens Output tokens Cost
Turn 1, write system prompt to cache Sonnet 2,000 (cache write) 150 $0.0098
Turns 2 to 10, cached system + new context Sonnet 9 × (2k cached + 1k new) 9 × 150 $0.0526
Follow-up question check, all 10 turns Haiku 4.5 10 × 400 10 × 50 $0.0065
End-of-response field extraction Sonnet 4,000 1,000 $0.0270
Per response, all in $0.096
Working number used for projections, $0.10 per completed response, with a few cents of slack for retries, abandoned half-responses we still partially pay for, and prompt drift over time.
Where money goes

Per-synthesis unit cost

A synthesis run feeds every response of one interview into Opus and gets back alignment, conflicts, and gaps. Cost scales with respondent count. Assumes 25 responses fed in at 800 tokens each plus a 5k orchestration prompt.

Step Model Input tokens Output tokens Cost
Full synthesis pass Opus 4 25,000 4,000 $0.675
Per synthesis run, all in $0.68
Synthesis is the chunky line item. Users typically re-run it 2 to 3 times as new responses land, so $1.50 per interview lifetime is a fair planning number at this respondent count.

Sticking at $19.99 is fine for the introduction offer window, both because it brings signups and because the overage slider is currently free. Once intro ends and overage starts billing at $1 per extra interview per month, that maps to roughly $0.10 a response, which is exactly cost recovery. No margin, no markup. That's a knob to revisit.
Reading these numbers

What I'd actually do

  • Hold the price through the introduction offer. Volume and signup velocity matter more than margin in the next 90 days.
  • Tighten abandoned-draft cleanup. The free funnel is the only line that scales linearly with signups without revenue. Even a small reduction in cost per free signup compounds.
  • Plan the Sonnet synthesis A/B. If Sonnet 4 holds quality, switching the default reclaims 8 to 10 margin points without raising the price.
  • Wire the overage at $1 only once Pro hits 50 paying users. Below that the friction is more expensive than the recovered cost.
  • Forecast assuming a 20% annual API price drop. Anthropic's curve has been steeply down. Build the model so that's upside, not a load-bearing assumption.
Assumptions in one place
  • Pricing, Anthropic, June 2026: Sonnet 4 at $3 input, $15 output, cache read $0.30, cache write $3.75. Opus 4 at $15 input, $75 output. Haiku 4.5 at $1 input, $5 output.
  • Per response: 10 turns, 2k cached system, 1k new context per turn, 150 token output. End-of-response extraction at 4k input, 1k output.
  • Per synthesis: 25 responses fed in at 800 tokens each, 5k orchestration prompt, 4k output.
  • Typical Pro user: 4 interviews per month, 25 respondents per interview, 2 synthesis runs per interview.
  • Free funnel: 10x of paying users, average $0.30 per free signup in Anthropic spend.
  • Railway cost scales near linearly with paid users above the $20 baseline.
  • Clerk free up to 10k MAU, Resend free up to 3k emails per month.
  • USD throughout, no FX, no tax, no Stripe fees.
