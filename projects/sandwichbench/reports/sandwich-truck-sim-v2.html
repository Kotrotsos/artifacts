How it works

Customers arrive randomly (Poisson). They join one FIFO order line; the front person orders at the order window, then steps aside to the waiting area. Orders drop into a shared queue that the cooks pull from one at a time. When food is ready the customer is called to the single pickup window, collects, and leaves. If the crowd hits the cap, new arrivals balk (give up).
