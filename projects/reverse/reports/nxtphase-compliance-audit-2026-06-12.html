nxtphase.ai - Privacy, Cookie & Accessibility Audit

GDPR / AVG compliance, cookie handling, US data transfers, and WCAG 2.1 AA review

Target: https://nxtphase.ai  ·  Date: 12 June 2026  ·  Method: external black-box (recon + headless browser render)  ·  Authorization: site owner

Executive summary

nxtphase.ai is a Dutch-language Webflow marketing site for an AI consultancy (lang nl-NL), targeting MKB+ companies. It is a static site behind Cloudflare with a single contact form, so the technical attack surface is small and no exposed services, databases, or secrets were found. The meaningful risk is on the compliance and accessibility side.

The headline issue: the site processes personal data (contact form, business email) but has no privacy statement and no cookie statement at all. The footer "Privacy" and "Cookies" links are dead placeholders (href="#") that are also hidden with a hide CSS class, and every candidate policy URL returns 404. For a Netherlands-based controller this is a direct AVG (GDPR) Article 13 gap. On the positive side, the site runs no analytics or marketing trackers and loads no external Google Fonts, which avoids the most commonly fined EU transfer problems.

1. GDPR / AVG & privacy

High F-1 No privacy statement exists or is linked

WCAG n/a · AVG/GDPR Art. 13 · Pages affected: site-wide

The footer contains two legal links that go nowhere and are hidden:

<a href="#" class="footer_legal-link hide">Privacy</a>
<a href="#" class="footer_legal-link hide">Cookies</a>

Every common policy URL returns 404: /privacy, /privacybeleid, /privacyverklaring, /privacy-policy, /cookies, /cookiebeleid, /algemene-voorwaarden, /disclaimer. A Netherlands-established controller that collects names, company, email and free-text messages must provide the Article 13 information (identity of controller, purposes, legal basis, recipients/processors, retention, data-subject rights, complaint route to the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens). None of this is available to a visitor.

Fix: Publish a real privacyverklaring (and a short cookie statement), wire both footer links to them, and remove the hide class.

Medium F-3 No cookie statement; cookie set without disclosure

ePrivacy / Telecommunicatiewet art. 11.7a · Pages affected: site-wide

On every page load the site sets one cookie, _cfuvid (Cloudflare, httpOnly, session). Verified in a real browser render: no other cookies, no consent banner, and none of the words "cookie/consent/akkoord" appear anywhere in the page body. _cfuvid is arguably a strictly-necessary cookie (Cloudflare bot/rate-limiting), so it likely does not need prior consent, which is why this is Medium and not High today. The gap is structural: there is no cookie statement informing users that any cookie is set, and there is no consent layer ready for the day analytics or marketing tags are added. The moment a Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, or LinkedIn Insight tag is added, the absence of a consent banner becomes a clear violation.

Fix: Add a brief cookie statement listing _cfuvid as functional/necessary. Before adding any analytics or marketing script, install a consent manager (e.g. Cookiebot, Complianz, Usercentrics) with prior-consent blocking.

Medium F-4 Contact-form "consent" is non-functional

AVG/GDPR Art. 13 & transparency · Page: /contact

Below the form sits the line "Ik ga akkoord met de verwerking van mijn gegevens om mijn aanvraag te behandelen." It is rendered as a static <div>, not a checkbox. The page contains zero checkbox elements, so the visitor never actively agrees, and the text does not link to any privacy information (there is none). It reads as if agreement is automatic on submit.

For a contact form the lawful basis is normally pre-contractual / legitimate interest, so an opt-in checkbox is not strictly required, but presenting an unchecked "I agree" statement with no privacy link is misleading and delivers none of the required transparency information.

Fix: Replace the sentence with a short notice that links to the privacy statement, e.g. "We gebruiken je gegevens alleen om je aanvraag te behandelen. Lees ons privacybeleid." If you want to rely on consent, make it a real, unticked checkbox.

Medium F-5 Contact form submits via GET

Privacy + minor security · Page: /contact

The form declares method="get". On submit, the name, company, email address, and the free-text message (up to 5000 chars) are placed into the URL query string. Query strings leak into browser history, the Referer header sent to any third party loaded next, proxy/server logs, and CDN access logs. This is data minimisation / confidentiality hygiene that should not be in a URL.

Fix: Change the form to method="post" (Webflow's default is POST/AJAX; this form was changed away from it).

2. US servers & data transfers

Medium F-6 EU personal data processed on US infrastructure, undisclosed

AVG/GDPR Ch. V (Art. 44-49) · Pages affected: site-wide

The site and the contact form run on US-headquartered processors. Confirmed signals:

WebflowHosting + form handlingx-wf-region: us-east-1, x-lambda-id, www CNAME cdn.webflow.comUS (AWS us-east-1)

Contact-form submissions therefore land on Webflow's US servers. This is lawful if each processor relies on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or Standard Contractual Clauses and the controller has a data processing agreement in place, but it must be disclosed in the privacy statement (which is missing) and recorded in the register of processing activities. Calendly is reached only when a visitor actively clicks the booking link, so it is not loaded on page view.

Fix: Sign/confirm DPAs with Webflow, Microsoft, Cloudflare, Slater (and Calendly if used), verify each is DPF-certified or covered by SCCs, and list them as recipients with the transfer basis in the privacy statement.

Good: No Google Analytics, Google Tag Manager, Meta Pixel, LinkedIn Insight, Hotjar, or Microsoft Clarity were found, and fonts are self-hosted via the Webflow CDN rather than loaded from fonts.googleapis.com. This avoids the exact "Google Fonts / Google Analytics transfers your IP to the US" issues that EU DPAs have repeatedly acted on. It also means there is currently no non-essential cookie that legally requires a consent banner.

3. Accessibility (WCAG 2.1 AA)

High F-2 No "skip to content" link

WCAG 2.4.1 Bypass Blocks (Level A) · Pages affected: site-wide

No skip link is present on any page. Keyboard and screen-reader users must tab through the entire navigation on every page before reaching content. This is a Level A failure.

Fix: Add a visually-hidden "Naar inhoud" link as the first focusable element that targets the main content landmark and becomes visible on focus.

Medium F-7 Links with no accessible name

WCAG 2.4.4 Link Purpose / 4.1.2 Name, Role, Value (Level A) · Page: home + footer

Three links on the homepage have no text, no aria-label, and no image alt, so a screen reader announces only "link":

  • Footer LinkedIn social icon: <a href="https://www.linkedin.com/company/nxt-phase-ai/" target="_blank"> (icon only)
  • Header logo link and footer logo link (inline SVG, no title/label)

Fix: Add aria-label="LinkedIn" to the social link and aria-label="Nxt Phase AI - home" to the logo links (or give the logo SVG a <title>).

Medium F-8 /contact page has no h1

WCAG 1.3.1 Info & Relationships (A) / 2.4.6 Headings (AA) · Page: /contact

The contact page heading structure starts at h2 with no h1. Screen-reader users navigating by heading lose the top-level page title. (The homepage is correct: exactly one h1 and a logical h2/h3 order.)

Fix: Promote the main contact heading to h1.

Medium F-9 Insufficient color contrast on consent text

WCAG 1.4.3 Contrast Minimum (Level AA) · Page: /contact (and any green body text)

The consent line uses Signal Green text on the cream background at 12px:

#3e9b5d text on #f9f6f1 background → contrast ratio 3.2:1 (computed: rgb(62,155,93) on rgb(249,246,241), font-size 12px).

AA requires 4.5:1 for normal-size text. At 12px this is normal text, so it fails. The same green used anywhere for normal-size body copy or links would also fail. (Green used as a background behind dark/white text, e.g. the "Verstuur" button, is fine.)

Fix: Darken the green for text use to roughly #2f7d49 or darker (4.5:1+ on cream), or increase the consent text size/weight. Keep the bright green for fills and large headings only.

Good (accessibility): every <img> has an alt attribute (decorative images correctly use empty alt); the homepage has exactly one h1 with a logical heading order; the contact form fields all have associated <label for> elements; and lang="nl-NL" is set. These are the fundamentals and they are done right.

4. Hardening & info disclosure (low)

Low F-10 Missing security headers

Defense-in-depth · Pages affected: site-wide

Present: Strict-Transport-Security (good), HTTPS enforced via 301. Missing: Content-Security-Policy, X-Content-Type-Options, X-Frame-Options/frame-ancestors, Referrer-Policy, Permissions-Policy. Webflow limits how much you can set, but Referrer-Policy: strict-origin-when-cross-origin is worth adding because it also reduces leakage of the GET form URL (see F-5).

Low F-11 Internal identifiers in response headers

Info disclosure · Pages affected: site-wide

Responses expose Webflow internals: surrogate-key with site/page IDs (6a155bcd...), x-lambda-id, and x-wf-region. These are normal Webflow platform headers and not directly exploitable; noted only for completeness. No API keys, tokens, or secrets were found in the HTML or JS bundles.

Recommendations

Immediate (this week)

  • Publish a privacyverklaring and a short cookie statement; wire the two footer links to them and remove the hide class. (F-1)
  • Change the contact form to method="post". (F-5)
  • Replace the static "Ik ga akkoord" text with a notice that links to the privacy statement. (F-4)
  • Add a skip-to-content link and fix the unnamed LinkedIn/logo links. (F-2, F-7)

Short term (this month)

  • Add an h1 to /contact; darken the green used for text to reach 4.5:1. (F-8, F-9)
  • Confirm DPAs and DPF/SCC coverage for Webflow, Microsoft 365, Cloudflare, Slater, Calendly; disclose them as processors. (F-6)
  • Add Referrer-Policy (and other headers where Webflow allows). (F-10)

Before adding any analytics or marketing tag

  • Install a consent manager with prior-consent blocking. Today there is no banner because there are no non-essential cookies; that changes the instant a tracker is added. (F-3)
