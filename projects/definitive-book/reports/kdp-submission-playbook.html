KDP Submission Playbook

From Vibe to Production: The Definitive Guide to Shipping Real AI Agents — everything you need to publish the eBook and the paperback yourself, in order.

You do the clicking; this page gives you every value to paste and every file to upload. Publish the eBook first, then add the paperback as a linked format so they share one product page. Budget ~30–45 minutes.

0. Before you start one-time

  1. Sign in at kdp.amazon.com.
  2. If this is your first title, finish account setup under your account menu: the tax interview and bank/deposit details. Titles cannot be created until this is done.
  3. Have these files on hand (full paths below in section 5).

Use the editor's B button on the first line and i on the book title. The text:
A working demo is not a shipped agent.

You can wire up an AI agent in an afternoon. Getting it to run reliably in front of real users, every time, while you sleep, is a different discipline. This book is that discipline.

From Vibe to Production walks the entire gap between a prototype that works once and an agent you can trust in production: the architecture that holds up, the evaluations that prove it works, the deployment that survives its first incident, and the team and business that grow around it.

Inside, you will learn how to:
- Strip an agent to the parts you can debug: a model, its tools, and a loop, without the framework cargo cult
- Design the harness that turns a clever prompt into a reliable system
- Decide when a capability should be a tool, a skill, or a subagent
- Build an eval suite and wire it into CI so you catch regressions before users do
- Draw the trust boundary between what runs unsupervised and what a human signs off
- Ship a managed agent, decide whether you actually need multi-agent, and run an AI-native team
- Find the durable advantage when everyone has the same models

Written for engineers and engineering leaders building agents right now, this is a practitioner's field guide, not a survey. Eighteen chapters, each ending with concrete Ship Notes you can act on the same day.

If you have shipped a demo that impressed the room and then wondered what it would take to put it in production, start here.

HTML version (if you publish via a tool that takes HTML) is stored in .claude/kdp.local.md under kdp.blurb.

Tab 2 — Kindle eBook Content

  1. Manuscript: Upload eBook manuscript → choose from-vibe-to-production.epub.

  3. ISBN: not needed for eBook (Amazon assigns a free ASIN).
  4. Wait for conversion, then open the Kindle Previewer and spot-check a few chapters, the TOC links, and an image.

2. Add the Paperback

From the same title on your Bookshelf, choose Create Paperback (links it to the eBook so they share one product page). Most details copy over; verify them.

Paperback Details

Title, subtitle, author, description, keywords, categories: identical to the eBook (above). Confirm they carried over.

After upload, open the Print Previewer and check: title/spine alignment, margins not cut off, images legible in grayscale. The cover was built for exactly 360 pages; if the page count ever changes, the spine must be rebuilt.

Paperback Pricing

Printing cost (US, 6×9 B&W white, 360 pp): $1.00 fixed + 360 × $0.012 = $5.32. Minimum list price for the 60% royalty is about $8.87.

3. After publishing

  • Claim your author page at authorcentral.amazon.com and paste the bio (section 5).
  • Verify the listing once live: “Look Inside,” category placement, the Buy button.
  • If you skipped KDP Select, the eBook can later be sold on other stores.
