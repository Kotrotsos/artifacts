RAG Triad evals opzetten

De drie LLM-as-judge evaluators aanzetten in Langfuse

Langfuse: langfuse.montferland.info · branch evals/rag-triad · bronbestanden in eval/rag-triad/

De drie judge-prompts staan al in Langfuse (namespace eval/, label production). Wat hier overblijft is eenmalig handwerk: per eval een Evaluator aanmaken in de UI. Dat kan niet via de API, want het evaluator-endpoint geeft 404 op deze Langfuse-versie. Doorloop stap 1 tot en met 5, en herhaal voor elk van de drie evals onderaan.

1 Nieuwe evaluator

  1. Open Langfuse, linkermenu Evaluations (of Evaluators / LLM-as-a-judge), dan Set up evaluator / New.
  2. Kies Custom (geen kant-en-klaar sjabloon).
  3. Plak de prompttekst van de betreffende eval (zie hieronder, knop Kopieer). Kan jouw versie naar een beheerde prompt verwijzen, kies dan de prompt eval/<naam> zodat het in sync blijft met de repo.
  4. Judge-model: gpt-4.1-mini (of gpt-4o-mini), temperature 0.

2 Score uit de output halen

Elke prompt geeft {"score": <0..1>, "reasoning": "<Nederlands>"} terug.

VeldInstelling
Score-naamcontext_relevance / groundedness / answer_relevance
TypeNumeric, bereik 0 tot 1
Mappingoutputveld score → de score, reasoning → het commentaar

3 Variabelen koppelen belangrijkste stap

Koppel elke {{variabele}} uit de prompt aan de juiste plek op een chat-trace:

VariabeleKoppel aanToelichting
{{query}}trace inputde vraag van de gebruiker
{{generation}}trace outputhet antwoord van de assistent
{{context}}de output van de RAG-zoekobservatiede opgehaalde chunks, alleen Context Relevance en Groundedness gebruiken dit

{{context}} vinden: de opgehaalde chunks zijn de output van de RAG-zoektool-observatie in de trace (de tool waarvan de backend het resultaat parseert in ChatHttpRoute.extractRagMetadata, bewaard als ragResults[].chunks). Kies in de variabele-mapping die observatie-output. Toont jouw versie alleen input/output op trace-niveau, dan kan Answer Relevance (heeft geen context nodig) sowieso al draaien.

4 Filter en sampling

  1. Filter op de chat-traces die je wilt, bijvoorbeeld environment production (of development tijdens testen) en/of trace-naam chat. Draai niet op losse, ongerelateerde traces.
  2. Sampling: begin laag (10 tot 20 procent) om judge-kosten te beperken, verhoog als de scores kloppen.
  3. Scope: nieuwe traces vanaf nu, eventueel een backfill over recente traces.

5 Controle

Open na het aanzetten een paar gescoorde traces en lees de reasoning. Score en uitleg horen te kloppen bij dat antwoord. Klopt iets niet, pas de prompt aan in eval/rag-triad/prompts/, draai bash eval/rag-triad/push-eval-prompts.sh opnieuw, en zet de evaluator op de nieuwe promptversie.

De drie evals

1. Context Relevance eval/context-relevance

Heeft retrieval het juiste materiaal gevonden? Variabelen: {{query}}, {{context}}.

You are an expert evaluator for a Dutch municipal (gemeente) RAG assistant.
Your job is to judge CONTEXT RELEVANCE: whether the retrieved context contains
the information needed to answer the user's question. You judge the RETRIEVAL
step only, not the final answer.

User question:
{{query}}

Retrieved context (chunks from the knowledge base):
{{context}}

Score how relevant the retrieved context is to answering the question, on a
continuous scale from 0.0 to 1.0:

- 1.0  The context directly and fully contains the information needed to answer
       the question, with no important gaps.
- 0.7  The context is on topic and contains most of what is needed, but misses a
       detail or includes some unrelated material.
- 0.4  The context is only partially relevant: a related municipal topic, but it
       does not actually contain the answer.
- 0.1  The context is off topic, or about a different service or procedure.
- 0.0  No usable context was retrieved (empty, or "no documents found").

Guidance:
- Judge relevance to the QUESTION, not the correctness of any answer.
- Dutch and English content are both acceptable, do not penalize language.
- Be strict: retrieving a lot of text that does not answer the question is not
  relevant.

Return ONLY a JSON object, with no other text:
{"score": <number between 0 and 1>, "reasoning": "<one or two sentences in Dutch explaining the score>"}

2. Groundedness eval/groundedness

Is het antwoord gedekt door dat materiaal (geen hallucinatie)? Variabelen: {{context}}, {{generation}}.

You are an expert evaluator for a Dutch municipal (gemeente) RAG assistant.
Your job is to judge GROUNDEDNESS (faithfulness): whether every factual claim in
the assistant's answer is supported by the retrieved context. This detects
hallucination.

Retrieved context (the only source the answer may rely on):
{{context}}

Assistant answer:
{{generation}}

Score how well the answer is grounded in the context, on a continuous scale from
0.0 to 1.0:

- 1.0  Every factual claim in the answer is directly supported by the context.
- 0.7  Mostly grounded: one minor detail is not explicit in the context but is a
       reasonable paraphrase of it.
- 0.4  Partially grounded: the answer mixes supported claims with claims that are
       not in the context.
- 0.1  Largely ungrounded: key claims (facts, amounts, deadlines, procedures,
       phone numbers, URLs) are not supported by the context.
- 0.0  The answer contradicts the context, or invents information wholesale.

Guidance:
- Generic, non-factual phrasing (greetings, "neem contact op met de gemeente",
  safe refusals, a clarifying question) counts as grounded.
- Specific facts that are not present in the context are hallucinations and
  lower the score.
- Do not reward correctness from world knowledge: only the provided context
  counts as support.

Return ONLY a JSON object, with no other text:
{"score": <number between 0 and 1>, "reasoning": "<one or two sentences in Dutch explaining the score, naming any unsupported claim>"}

3. Answer Relevance eval/answer-relevance

Beantwoordt het antwoord de vraag? Variabelen: {{query}}, {{generation}}. Heeft geen context nodig, kan meteen draaien.

You are an expert evaluator for a Dutch municipal (gemeente) RAG assistant.
Your job is to judge ANSWER RELEVANCE: whether the assistant's answer actually
addresses the user's question and is useful to a citizen.

User question:
{{query}}

Assistant answer:
{{generation}}

Score how well the answer addresses the question, on a continuous scale from 0.0
to 1.0:

- 1.0  Directly and completely answers the question, a citizen knows what to do
       next.
- 0.7  Answers the main question but is incomplete or slightly off, or buries the
       answer.
- 0.4  Partially relevant: touches the topic but does not really answer it, or
       answers a different question.
- 0.1  Evasive or generic ("neem contact op met de gemeente") when the question
       could have been answered.
- 0.0  Off topic, empty, or refuses a reasonable, answerable question.

Guidance:
- A correct clarifying question for a genuinely ambiguous query scores high
  (0.8 to 1.0): it is the relevant response.
- A correct "ik kan dit niet beantwoorden" for an out-of-scope question scores
  high: refusing the unanswerable is relevant.
- Judge relevance and usefulness, not factual correctness or grounding (those
  are covered by the other two evaluators).

Return ONLY a JSON object, with no other text:
{"score": <number between 0 and 1>, "reasoning": "<one or two sentences in Dutch explaining the score>"}
Lees de drie samen: lage Context Relevance wijst op retrieval, hoge Context Relevance maar lage Groundedness wijst op de generatiestap die dingen verzint, hoog op de eerste twee maar lage Answer Relevance wijst op een correct maar onbruikbaar antwoord. Bron: eval/rag-triad/SETUP.md en README.md.
