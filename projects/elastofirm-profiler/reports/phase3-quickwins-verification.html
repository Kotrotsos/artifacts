▶ INC-89

Sort similarity by surface instead of weight

M Verified (logic) In Review

Adds a "Sort by surface (area) similarity" toggle on image-search results. Surface is the geometry-true measure (independent of compound density). Mirrors the existing weight rerank and is mutually exclusive with it.

Touched: app/page.tsx (prioritizeArea state, area rerank, toggle, deps/effect)

Verify visually
  1. Open http://localhost:3001 and upload a profile image, then run the image search.
  2. In the results header, flip Sort by surface (area) similarity.
  3. Expected: results reorder so those closest in surface to the top match rise; toggling weight off when area is on (mutually exclusive).

Note: image-search only. Verified by compile + logic-mirror of the proven weight rerank; full reorder needs a real image upload to confirm (not covered by the API suite).
