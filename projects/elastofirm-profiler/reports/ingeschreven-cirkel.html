Ingeschreven cirkel

In the context of rubber profile manufacturing and curing

The ingeschreven cirkel (inscribed circle) is the largest circle that fits inside the cross-section of a rubber profile. It represents the thickest internal point of the shape.

Why it matters for heating and curing

  1. Cure time is determined by the thickest cross-section, because heat has to penetrate to the center.
  2. The inscribed circle diameter gives the worst-case heat conduction distance.
  3. Larger inscribed circle, longer vulcanization time at a given temperature.
  4. It is a standard input for calculating extrusion line speed, dwell time in the curing oven, and salt-bath, UHF, or microwave cure parameters.

In practice: if your profile has a 3 mm inscribed circle, the rubber compound in that thickest spot needs enough oven time to reach cure temperature throughout. Speed up the line too much and you get undercure at the core.

Some companies use the inscribed circle as a design rule, keeping it below a target diameter so the cure cycle stays economical.
