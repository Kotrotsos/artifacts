The problem

Gathering answers from people is broken at both ends

Forms

Cheap, but shallow

People skim, skip, and abandon. A form can never ask the obvious follow-up. The most valuable answer is the one it didn't think to ask for.

Interviews

Rich, but don't scale

A live conversation gets the truth, but you can't book thirty calls this week, and certainly not a thousand. So everyone settles.

The insight

The magic of an interview isn't the human

It's the follow-up question, the adaptation, the listening. AI can now do that part convincingly, for every respondent, at the same time. The scarce resource just stopped being scarce.
